Common questions

How do I change my email address?

Go to Settings, update your email under Account, and select Save changes. We'll send a confirmation link to the new address.

How do I report a post?

Open the post and use the menu in its top corner to select Report. A moderator reviews it within 24 hours.

Can I hide my profile from non-members?

Yes. Profiles are only visible to signed-in members of your allotment group.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, select Forgot password and follow the link we email you.
